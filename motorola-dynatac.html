1983: Motorola DynaTac 8000X

The Motorola DynaTAC 8000X was the first mobile phone. It came out in 1983. It was big and heavy, about the size of a brick, and weighed almost 2 pounds. People called it a "brick phone" because of its size and shape.

The phone had buttons to dial numbers and a small screen to show the numbers you typed. It could only make calls and had no other features like texting or apps. The battery lasted about 30 minutes of talking before needing a recharge, which took 10 hours.

The DynaTAC 8000X was very expensive, costing about $4,000 when it first came out. Only wealthy people or businesses could afford it. Even though it was large and costly, it was the start of mobile phones. Over time, phones got smaller, cheaper, and better because of this first step in technology.
