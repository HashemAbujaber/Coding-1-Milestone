2000: Sharp J-SH04

The Sharp J-SH04 was released in 2000 and was one of the first mobile phones with a built-in camera. It had a small 0.1-megapixel camera, allowing users to take pictures, which was a new feature at the time. The phone also had a small color screen where users could view photos and text messages.

The J-SH04 could send pictures via email or text, making it one of the first phones to support photo messaging. It had a flip design, making it easy to carry around and use.

The phone was popular in Japan and helped start the trend of camera phones. It was an important step in the development of smartphones, showing how mobile phones could be used for more than just making calls. The Sharp J-SH04 paved the way for future phones with better cameras and features.
