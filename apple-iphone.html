2007: Apple iPhone

The Apple iPhone was first released in 2007. It was a big change in phones. It had a touchscreen, so you could use your fingers instead of buttons. The screen was big, which made it easier to see things.

You could use the internet, check emails, and listen to music, all on one device. It also had a camera to take pictures. The iPhone used apps, which were small programs for things like games, maps, and social media. This made it very useful for everyday life.

People liked how easy it was to use. The iPhone looked modern, with a sleek design. Steve Jobs showed the iPhone to the world in a big presentation. It was very popular and sold millions of units. The 2007 iPhone changed how people use phones and started the smartphone era.
