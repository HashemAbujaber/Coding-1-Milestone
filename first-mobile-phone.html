1973: First Mobile Phone

The first mobile phone was created in 1973. It was very large and heavy, weighing about 2.5 pounds. The phone was not like the small phones we use today. It allowed people to make calls without needing a wire or a landline, which was a big change during that time.

The phone took 10 hours to charge fully, but it could only be used for 30 minutes before needing another charge. Even though it had many limits, it was still an important invention. It showed that talking to people from different places without wires was possible.

Source: Sean Macaulay & Bob Green, thedayintech.com
